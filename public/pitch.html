The problem

The house is trapped.
The night is not a market.

01

Ownership is the whole deed

A buyer who wants the yield has to buy the building. There is no clean fraction, and no standard way to hold it beside everything else in a wallet.

02

Bookings never settle on-chain

Guests pay an operator. The calendar, the payment, and the right to stay live in three different systems. None of them can be resold.

03

Nothing composes

A week in a villa cannot be collateral. A share of the rent cannot sit in another protocol. The asset ends where the property company ends.

The insight

A place is two products.
The building earns.
The night is used.

They should not be the same token. One legal owner. Two rights. Issued where payments already clear.

WayFinder

Wrap the title.
Issue the rights.

Each asset sits in a local SPV. Token holders own economic rights in that vehicle, not the deed. Night rights are a license to occupy, with blackout dates and house rules.

Ownership · yield

Fractions

A Token-2022 share of the SPV. It claims net operating yield and can be posted as collateral without giving the yield up.

Usage · exclusive

Nights

One token, one night, the whole property. Book it, sell it, or redeem it. It is not equity, so it stays out of the vault.

How it works

Four moves.
Then it composes.

01

A local SPV holds the title or the operating lease. One vehicle per asset.

02

03

04

Compose

Fractions lock in the vault, keep accruing yield, and borrow against a 55% LTV.
